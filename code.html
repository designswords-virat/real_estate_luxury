Materials & Provenance

The honesty of raw materials, sourced at the source.

Every Aetheris residence is composed of a curated palette of natural materials, each documented with a verified provenance certificate.

01 · Stone

Travertine

Cut from the Tivoli quarries outside Rome. Hand-honed in our Zurich finishing workshop.

02 · Timber

Smoked Oak

Slow-smoked over white-oak chips in our Tokyo atelier. Hand-finished with raw linseed oil.

03 · Metal

Patinated Bronze

Cast at a single foundry in Bologna. Aged for 90 days outdoors before final installation.

04 · Glass

Linen Glass

A brushed-linen weave glass produced by a single family-run furnace in Murano since 1894.
